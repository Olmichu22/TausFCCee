\section{Executive summary}

The large global W/P8O photon difference is driven primarily by sample/generator truth composition and truth-to-PFO association/reconstructability, not by a large change in conditional reconstructed photon PID. Tau topology, selected visible truth from tau decays, tau-decay photon kinematics, reconstructed PFO composition, and conditional PID are closely compatible between W and P8O.

The validated factorization is
\begin{center}
\fbox{\small origin/composition $\rightarrow$ phase space $\rightarrow$ unique PFO association $\rightarrow$ conditional PID $\rightarrow$ global outcome}.
\end{center}

P8O contains 49,120 photons with positive stored genealogy evidence for \code{explicit\_ISR}; they are exceptionally soft and beam-collinear. W contains 25,879 parentless non-tau photons without stored photon-to-beam ancestry. They remain \code{parentless\_unresolved}; only ``partially similar'' or ``ISR-like kinematics'' is allowed. Direct tau daughters remain \code{tau\_direct\_daughter\_unresolved}: the records do not support a positive-evidence, cross-generator FSR definition.

P8O has 49,120 selected photons with a same-sign electron-copy ancestry chain ending on parentless incoming beam $e^-/e^+$ objects with approximately $E=45.595$ GeV and $|\cos\theta|=0.9999755$. This is positive evidence for \code{explicit\_ISR}.

All 25,879 W non-tau photons are parentless. Beam objects exist, but no photon-to-beam ancestry is stored; they remain \code{parentless\_unresolved}. \section{Frozen scientific interpretation}

The large global W/P8O photon difference is primarily driven by the different non-tau photon populations and their phase-space composition. P8O contains an explicit-ISR population that is substantially softer and more beam-collinear than the unresolved W parentless population. Tau-decay photon populations have very similar truth kinematics and PFO association efficiencies. Once a unique PFO exists, conditional photon PID is very similar across samples and origins. The global migration difference is therefore dominated by \textbf{sample/generator truth composition plus truth-to-PFO association/reconstructability}, not a large difference in reconstructed photon PID.

\section{Explicit limitations and freeze declaration}

\begin{enumerate}
\item W \code{parentless\_unresolved} photons cannot be called ISR.
\item P8O \code{explicit\_ISR} is supported by stored generator genealogy.
\item \code{tau\_direct\_daughter\_unresolved} photons cannot currently be called FSR.
\item G/L\_direct/L\_ancestor are association semantics, not detector definitions.
\item This is not a generator-physics validation of WHIZARD versus PYTHIA8.
\item Raw all-photon outcomes cannot establish that one reconstruction globally reconstructs photons better.
\item Equal-statistics detailed audits use only 18,000 events per sample.
\end{enumerate}

These are limitations of stored event-record information, not pending software bugs.
